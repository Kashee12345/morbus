# MORBUS — The Descent of Dr. Hill

**A real-time, Diablo-style dungeon crawler where the monsters are diseases, and you win by matching the treatment to the mechanism.**

### ▶ [Play it now: kashee12345.github.io/morbus](https://kashee12345.github.io/morbus/)

Runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop, tablet or phone. Nothing to install, no account, no ads. Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## The story

> In the valley of Asclepion stands the **Sanctuary Infirmary**, the last house of healing for a hundred leagues. For a century its lamps never went dark.
>
> Then, beneath its oldest ward, in catacombs dug before medicine had a name, something woke. It wasn't a demon of fire and brimstone. It was a demon of *mechanism*, and it named itself **SEPSIS, the Prime Malady**.
>
> It sent three lieutenants into the body of the world. **INFARCTUS** seized the heart. **PNEUMOTHRAX** crushed the lungs. **CEREBRYX** swelled within the mind. One by one, the systems began to fail.
>
> Brave healers went down with courage and steel. None returned, because the maladies obey only one law: *treat the mechanism, not the symptom.* Antibiotics cannot kill a virus. tPA cannot melt a plaque. A shock that saves a heart will feed a seizure.
>
> One physician remains who knows the difference: **Dr. Jonathan Hill**, in navy scrubs, with a scalpel and a satchel of antibiotics, plasmin and lightning.
>
> *The lamps are going out, Doctor. Descend.*

## The four acts

| Act | Realm | System | Boss | Signature mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | The Clotted Cathedral | Cardiovascular | 🫀 **INFARCTUS**, Butcher of the LAD | Charges, cleaves, and leaves *ischemic ground* behind. Time is muscle. |
| II | The Drowned Alveoli | Respiratory | 🫁 **PNEUMOTHRAX**, the Collapsing King | Pressure builds toward a lethal *TENSION* blast. Grab the 14-gauge needle and decompress him. |
| III | The Halls of the Infarct | Neurologic | 🧠 **CEREBRYX**, the Swelling Mind | Intracranial pressure rises toward herniation (Cushing's triad). Stand in the *mannitol* fonts. |
| IV | The Septic Throne | Systemic & Immune | ☠️ **SEPSIS**, the Prime Malady | Three phases: SIRS → septic shock (keep MAP ≥ 65 in the *norepinephrine* fonts) → multi-organ failure. |

Each act has two procedurally generated dungeon floors and a boss lair. Every run has a different layout.

## New player? Follow the walkthrough

A **📜 Walkthrough** panel in the top-left corner guides you one step at a time, and a **golden arrow** points to where you need to go:

1. Talk to Charge Nurse Ada, then Elder Pathologist Morrow.
2. Take the stairs north into the dungeon.
3. Learn to walk and attack, then read a monster's weakness by hovering over it.
4. Kill a bacterium with antibiotics (**1**), dissolve a clot with tPA (**2**), and shock a V-Fib Wraith (**3**).
5. Drink a potion (**Q**), equip loot, answer a Shrine of Knowledge, spend skill points (**K**), and find the stairs.

After that, the panel switches to the **current boss objective** with a strategy tip. The full written walkthrough, including every boss strategy, is under **Help (H)**. You can skip or hide any step.

**Survival tips:** monsters flash a red ring just before they strike, so step back when you see it. Press **Q** when life drops below half; the screen will remind you. Press **T** to portal home, and Ada heals you for free. You start with *Dr. Hill's Lucky Scalpel*, 212 life and 8 potions.

### The one rule that wins the game

**Hover over any monster to read its weakness.** Every enemy is a real disease mechanism, and your four skills are real treatment classes:

- 💊 **Antibiotics** shred bacteria (Staph, Pneumococcus, Meningococcus) but do **nothing** to the Influenza Wisp, because viruses don't have cell walls.
- 🌀 **tPA** dissolves fibrin clots (emboli, ischemic strokes). It barely scratches the **Plaque Ghoul** (cholesterol isn't fibrin) and is useless against the **DIC Swarm** (you never give fibrinolytics in DIC).
- ⚡ **Defibrillation** destroys the V-Fib Wraith, but it **heals** the Seizure Spark, because a seizure is neurons firing, not a cardiac rhythm.
- 🛡️ **Corticosteroids** quench inflammation: mast cells, cytokine storms, vasogenic edema.

The first time you hit an immunity or resistance, a short lesson card explains why. The **Codex (X)** keeps a bestiary of everything you've met.

### Loot, shrines and identifying items

- Items come in five tiers: <span>white</span> normal, **blue** magic, **yellow** rare, **gold** unique and **green** set. Affixes are named after real drugs (*Vancomycin*, *Tenecteplase*, *Amiodarone*, *Dexamethasone*…) and each tooltip teaches what that drug does.
- Uniques honor medical history: *Lister's Carbolic Blade*, *Fleming's Moldy Signet*, *Laennec's Hollow Tube*, *Semmelweis's Clean Hands*, and more. Collect all three pieces of **Virchow's Triad** (stasis · hypercoagulability · endothelial injury) for the set bonus.
- Rare and better items drop **unidentified**. Click one to **run the labs**: answer a board-style question to reveal it, or pay the Elder Pathologist in town.
- **Shrines of Knowledge** ask a pathophysiology question. Answer correctly for a blessing (Fury, Vigor, Fortune, Insight or Restoration). Every answer shows the rationale.

There are 48 questions in total, 12 per body system, covering Virchow's triad, troponin, Beck's triad, the tension pneumothrax, Cushing's triad, CSF patterns, Sepsis-3, the hour-1 bundle, DIC labs and more.

### The town: the Sanctuary Infirmary

- **Charge Nurse Ada** fully heals you.
- **Apothecary Wren** sells potions and gear, and buys your loot.
- **Elder Pathologist Morrow** identifies items and briefs you on the next boss.
- **The Waypoint** lets you jump to any floor you've reached.

### After the credits

Beat SEPSIS to unlock **Nightmare** difficulty, where the maladies are tougher and the loot is better.

### Secret

Type **`brooks`** on the title screen, then start a New Descent, for *Legend Mode*: level 15, the full Virchow's Triad set, Lister's Blade, and every waypoint unlocked. It's handy for demos.

## Technical notes

- One self-contained `index.html` file. All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized live, so there are no image or audio files.
- Works offline once loaded (the fonts fall back gracefully).
- Save data lives in your browser's localStorage, so clearing site data resets your progress.
